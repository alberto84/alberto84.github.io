abstract: The rapid growth of virtual environments in the Metaverse makes it increasingly important to adopt reliable and privacy-preserving mechanisms for authenticating avatars before social, economic, or collaborative interactions take place. Current solutions for managing identities in the Metaverse are account-based and rely on credentials such as passwords, tokens, or decentralized identifiers managed through digital wallets. While these methods offer portability and interoperability, they do not establish a solid link between the virtual identity and a single physical source of trust. Therefore, they remain vulnerable to impersonation attacks, credential theft, and identity duplication. This paper proposes the Metaverse PUF-anchored Digital Identity Card (MetaPUF-ID) for avatars, based on the integration of Physically Unclonable Functions (PUFs) and Blockchain, with the aim of establishing a verifiable and unclonable identity layer for Metaverse avatars. In this framework, PUFs are used as an authentication primitive because they exploit physical randomness, generate highly distinctive responses, and support robust identification properties that are difficult to replicate or reverse-engineer, whereas Blockchain provides a decentralized and tamper-resistant infrastructure for storing and managing identity credentials.
